Accessibility Tips

  • Press ⏸ Motion to disable all animations (seizure safety)
  • Press ⬛ Contrast for high-contrast mode
  • Press A+ / A− to resize text
  • Press 🎯 Focus to hide sidebars
  • Use Tab to navigate by keyboard
  • Line chart is gentler on eyes than candlestick
  • The data table below the chart works with screen readers
